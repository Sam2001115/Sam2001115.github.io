// Engineering Challenges
01 — GPIO9 Strapping Pin / Fan PWM Unusable

GPIO9 on the ESP32-C3 is a strapping pin tied to boot mode selection — cannot be driven LOW as a software output. Three approaches attempted before confirming the hardware limitation.

→ SOLUTION: Physical slide switch on 5V fan power line.
02 — ADC1 Pin Shortage

Only ADC1 channels exposed on ESP32-C3 SuperMini. With GPIO0, GPIO1, and GPIO20 already assigned, no free ADC1 pin remained for solar voltage monitoring.

→ SOLUTION: Solar voltage mirrors battery in v1. ADS1115 I2C ADC planned for v2.
03 — Relay Boot Flicker

GPIO pins float briefly before Arduino init on power-up, causing momentary relay energisation.

→ SOLUTION: ESP-IDF gpio_reset_pin() → gpio_set_direction() → gpio_set_level() before Arduino pinMode().
04 — ACS712 Zero-Current Offset Drift

Initial calibration at 1.56V produced a consistent -1.77A reading at zero current due to supply and temperature variation.

→ SOLUTION: Raw ADC calibration at no-load — midpoint corrected to 0.661V. ±0.15A deadband filter added.
05 — Voltage Divider Ratio Miscalculation

Theoretical ratio of 4.03 incorrect due to resistor tolerance stack-up in the physical build.

→ SOLUTION: Empirical calibration against multimeter — true ratio corrected to 23.0.
06 — DHT22 NaN on First Read

Sensor consistently returned NaN — requires stabilisation period after power-on.

→ SOLUTION: 2-second delay between Serial.begin() and dht.begin() in setup().
07 — TFT Display Ghosting

Partial-screen refresh left residual pixels from previous renders when switching text sizes.

→ SOLUTION: Per-zone fillRect() clearing before each redraw instead of full-screen clear.

System fully operational — real-time monitoring of current, voltage, power, temperature, and humidity.
Web dashboard live at 192.168.4.1 — relay control from any device, no router required.
Relay automation confirmed — load disconnect below 10.5V, charge enable when solar exceeds battery.
ACS712 stable at 0.00A no-load. Battery voltage accurate to ±0.1V vs multimeter.
TFT smooth — no ghosting or flicker across 3-screen navigation. All touch gestures functional.
Enclosure designed in SolidWorks — split body for 3D printing, ventilation grille, all connector cutouts.
